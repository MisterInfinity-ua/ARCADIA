BUILT FOR PLAYERS, BY PLAYERS

ARCADIA is a passion project — a love letter to the golden age of arcade gaming, rebuilt entirely in HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. No frameworks, no servers, no nonsense.

Every game is hand-coded from scratch with pixel-perfect care: smart AI opponents, responsive controls, upgrade systems, and full color customization. The goal? Give you the most fun possible with zero friction.
