CONTROLS & HOW TO PLAY

⌨️
KEYBOARD CONTROLS
Use WASD / Arrow Keys for movement, Spacebar to jump, shoot or drop, and 1-9 for weapon / hotbar selection.
🖱️
MOUSE & POINTER LOCK
In 3D games (Minecraft, DOOM), click to lock mouse look. Left-click to mine/fire, and right-click to place blocks or interact.
📱
TOUCH & MOBILE COMPATIBLE
On touchscreens and tablets, games feature on-screen directional controls, swipe gestures, and responsive tap controls.

ABOUT ARCADIA

ARCADIA is an open-source, ultra-fast browser gaming arcade built with modern HTML5, WebGL, Web Audio synthesis, and Three.js. It features zero tracking, no intrusive advertisements, no paywalls, and instant one-click gameplay for players worldwide.
